# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free community for
everyone participating in this project, regardless of experience, identity, or
background.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Assume good intent while taking responsibility for impact.
- Welcome questions and help others learn.
- Keep criticism focused on ideas, code, and observable behavior.
- Respect privacy and avoid posting credentials or personal information.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention.
- Trolling, sustained disruption, or deliberate intimidation.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  community.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions,
reviews, and other project spaces. It also applies when someone is representing
the project in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner using a private
contact method listed on the
[`@bproject07` GitHub profile](https://github.com/bproject07). Do not post
sensitive incident details in a public issue.

If no private maintainer contact is available, you may open a support issue that
only requests private contact, without incident details. For conduct that also
violates GitHub's policies, use
[GitHub's abuse reporting process](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse).

Maintainers will review reports as confidentially as practical, may request
additional context, and may remove content, issue a warning, temporarily limit
participation, or permanently ban participation depending on severity and
pattern. Retaliation against a reporter or participant in an investigation is
not permitted.

This policy is inspired by the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
